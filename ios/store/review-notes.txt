Silent Medic Vet 1.1 — notes for App Review

Thank you for the review of 1.0 (Guidelines 4.3(a)/(b) and 4.2.6). Version 1.1 is a substantial rework: the app now has its own native iPhone features, its own design and icon, and its own navigation. No reviewer account or network is needed; the app makes no network connections.

WHAT IS NEW AND NATIVE IN 1.1
1. Patients tab (Swift plugin PatientChartPlugin): on-device animal profiles (name or tag, species, a weight the user noted, notes, dated observation log), stored in a Data Protection file in the app's Application Support folder. "Chart this animal" sets the species throughout the app. A noted weight is a record only; the app never calculates a dose from it.
2. Recheck reminders (RecheckPlugin, UserNotifications): local notifications per patient. Tapping one opens that patient. No push, no server.
3. Spotlight (KnowledgeIndexPlugin, CoreSpotlight): every reference entry and condition card is indexed on the device; a Spotlight result opens that card in the app.
4. Siri and Shortcuts (App Intents on iOS 16+, App Shortcuts with Siri phrases on iOS 17+): "Look up a drug for my dog in Silent Medic Vet", "Build a triage plan for my horse in Silent Medic Vet", "Open my patients in Silent Medic Vet". The animal is a parameter.
5. Routing (FieldRouter): Spotlight activities, intents and reminder taps are delivered to the app through AppDelegate and a notification-center delegate.
6. Haptics (FieldHapticsPlugin): Taptic feedback on selections, plan building, saves and reminders.
7. New design: bottom tab bar (Reference, Conditions, Triage, Patients), evergreen / sage / copper colours, rounded type, new paw-and-cross icon and launch screen.

HOW TO TEST
- Launch: the knowledge base is decompressed and its SHA-256 integrity is checked; the opening screen then fades.
- Reference: pick Dog, search "ibuprofen", open the card ("Dog: do not use"). Switch to Veterinarian (top right) and pick Cattle to see withdrawal times.
- Conditions tab: open Glaucoma.
- Triage tab: tap Eye, Today, then Build plan.
- Patients tab: New patient > name "Bella", species Dog > Save. Tap Recheck reminder > In 4 hours (or pick a time a few minutes ahead) > Set reminder; allow notifications. Lock the phone; when the reminder arrives, tap it and the app opens at Bella. Add observation > Add to log.
- Spotlight: swipe down on the Home Screen and type "carprofen" or "glaucoma"; tap the Silent Medic Vet result.
- Siri / Shortcuts (iOS 17+): say "Look up a drug for my horse in Silent Medic Vet", or open the Shortcuts app > Silent Medic Vet.

CONTENT AND SAFETY
Doses are shown only as the label or published formulary states them, in Veterinarian view; nothing is calculated from an animal's weight (Guideline 1.4.2). Unapproved research peptides are not included in the App Store build. Every card carries red-flag signs and the advice to contact a veterinarian; the app states that it is not a diagnosis.

ORIGINALITY
Silent Medic Vet is not built from a template or app generator. Its knowledge base (89 entries, 12 condition cards, 13 species, rule-based triage plans) and its native Swift code were written for this app. It shares no code with other apps beyond Apple's frameworks and the open-source Capacitor runtime that hosts the app's interface.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no network requests. Patient profiles and reminders stay on the device. Privacy manifest included (UserDefaults, reason CA92.1).
