SILENT MEDIC VET v0.2.0 · build 2026-09-25 · knowledge base 94 entries across 6 treatment tiers plus human medicines at home · 12 conditions · 13 species.

All inference is local. No data leaves this device. Doses shown in Veterinarian mode are reference values from published formularies and labels; verify against current labels, Plumb's, and FARAD before use. Extra-label use in food animals requires a valid VCPR and a FARAD-derived withdrawal.

Unapproved peptides (Tier 3C) are summarized for reference only and are not recommended by this tool. Tier 7 lists human medicines an owner may already have; using a human prescription product in an animal is extra-label and requires a veterinarian's order.
